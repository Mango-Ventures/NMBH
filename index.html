About the Hospital

Where clinical excellence meets genuine care.

Nirshu Babu Memorial Hospital was built on a simple belief — the people of Begusarai deserve world-class treatment without travelling to distant cities. As the region's first NABH-accredited specialty hospital for Orthopaedics and Gynaecology, we combine national quality standards with the warmth of local care.

From complex bone and joint procedures to complete women's health and maternity services, our team is dedicated to safe, ethical and compassionate treatment — around the clock.

  • NABH accreditation — verified national standards of safety & hygiene.
  • Experienced specialists in Orthopaedics and Gynaecology.
  • Modern facilities — clean wards, diagnostics and operation theatre.
  • Transparent, fair treatment with a patient-first approach.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about location, timings, departments and appointments.

Where is Nirshu Babu Memorial Hospital located?
Nirshu Babu Memorial Hospital is at Har Har Mahadeo Chowk, near Alka Cinema, opposite Durga Mandir, Kapasiya, Begusarai, Bihar 851117.
What are the hospital timings? Is it open 24 hours?
Yes — the hospital is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year including Sundays and holidays, with a round-the-clock emergency team.
What is the contact number?
You can call or WhatsApp us on 074886 98630 to book an appointment or reach the emergency helpline.
Is the hospital NABH-accredited?
Yes. Nirshu Babu Memorial Hospital is the region's first NABH-accredited specialty hospital for Orthopaedics and Gynaecology — meaning it meets national standards for quality, safety and hygiene.
Which departments and treatments are available?
Orthopaedics (fractures, joint replacement, arthroscopy, spine care), Gynaecology & Obstetrics (pregnancy care, normal and C-section delivery, laparoscopic surgery), 24×7 Emergency & Trauma, Diagnostics & Pathology, General Medicine and Physiotherapy.
Does it provide emergency and ambulance services?
Yes — 24×7 emergency and trauma care with ambulance support for rapid response.
How can I book an appointment?
Call 074886 98630, send a WhatsApp message, or fill the appointment form on this website — it sends your request straight to the hospital on WhatsApp.
Does the hospital handle deliveries and maternity care?
Yes. The Gynaecology & Obstetrics department offers complete maternity care including antenatal check-ups, normal delivery and caesarean (C-section) delivery.
